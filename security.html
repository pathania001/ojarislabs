Security & Compliance

Security is built into everything we design, build and operate.

This page outlines our general security approach. Specific certifications and controls should be confirmed and documented before publishing as commitments.

Our Approach

We apply security-by-design principles across the software lifecycle — from architecture and code review to deployment and monitoring.

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
  • Least-privilege access and strong authentication.
  • Secure development practices and dependency management.
  • Continuous monitoring and incident response readiness.

Compliance

We help clients align their solutions with relevant standards and regulatory requirements. Applicable frameworks depend on your industry and region.

Report a Vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue, please contact us responsibly at security@ojarislabs.com.
