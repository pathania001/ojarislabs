FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does OjarisLabs do?

OjarisLabs is a digital engineering and growth partner. We design, build, automate and optimize websites, custom software, eCommerce stores, mobile apps and AI-powered workflows — connecting technology to real business outcomes.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with startups, growing businesses and established teams across industries — from a first website or online store to a custom SaaS platform or an internal automation project.

Can OjarisLabs handle both design and development?

Yes. We cover UI/UX and brand design through to front-end and back-end engineering, so strategy, design and build stay aligned end to end.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, monitoring, performance work and iterative improvements so your product keeps evolving after go-live.

Can you work with an existing website or software product?

Absolutely. We regularly take over, audit, extend and modernize existing codebases, sites and stores rather than always starting from scratch.

Do you provide AI and automation services?

Yes — practical, well-scoped AI integrations and workflow automation such as assistants, document processing, lead qualification and CRM automation, connected through APIs to your existing tools.

Can you work with remote and international clients?

Yes. We operate remote-first and collaborate with businesses across locations and time zones.
