Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

This is a template privacy policy provided as a starting point. Review and finalize with qualified legal counsel before publishing.

OjarisLabs ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you interact with our website and services.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone, company) when you submit a form.
  • Project information you choose to share with us.

How We Use Information
